The brain

Same brain as the chess page, trained on 9×9 Go: KataGo g170 self-play positions (CC0) — the fly learned to agree with KataGo's most-visited move 30% of the time (random: 3.5%) and to read final territory at 75% per point (coin flip: 50%). Its sense of who is winning never trained, so it plays on instinct alone. Encoder: the 10,855 photoreceptors are cut into a 9×9 grid on each eye's sheet; each board point drives its own patch. Connectome: FlyWire FAFB v783 (Dorkenwald et al. 2024; Schlegel et al. 2024; signs from Shiu et al. 2024) — non-commercial terms apply. Idea: ChessFly by Maxime Labonne. Source: NakliTechie/flymate.
